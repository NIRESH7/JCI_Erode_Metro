Privacy Policy

JCI Erode Metro — Last updated: July 2026

This policy describes how the JCI Erode Metro mobile app collects and uses information.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, phone, email, business profile, and photo.
  • Contacts when you explicitly pick a contact for referrals.
  • Device tokens for push notifications.
  • Usage data required for events, referrals, blood requests, and member features.

How we use information

We use data to authenticate members, operate referrals, send notifications, and display authorized member content. We do not sell personal data.

Data sharing

Data is shared with JCI Erode Metro administrators and infrastructure providers (hosting, Firebase/Google) as needed to operate the app.

Security & retention

API communication uses HTTPS. Login tokens are stored securely on your device. Data is retained while your membership is active.

Your choices

You may update your profile, log out, or request deletion by contacting support@jcierodegreencity.com.
